Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Creamer is a private tracker for adults. It stores what it needs to run your account and nothing else. There are no ads, no analytics and no selling of data.

In short: we keep your email, your username and the sessions you log. Only you and the friends you accept see your sessions. You can turn sharing off in Settings. You can copy your data, erase it or delete your account in Settings at any time.

Who runs Creamer

Creamer is an independent project run by one developer. For anything about your data, write to privacy@creamer.ink.

What we store

DataWhy
Email and passwordTo create your account and let you log in. Passwords are stored as a secure hash, never as plain text.
Username and bioSo friends can find and recognise you. The bio is optional and up to 80 characters.
SessionsFor each session: start and end date and time, length, rating, whether it was released, and any tags you add. This is the core of the app.
Friends, duels and crewsWho you are friends with, friend requests, duels you start or accept, and crews you join.
Settings and progressYour theme, sharing choices, LDs, badges, challenges and Shop items.

We do not collect photos, contacts, location, or device identifiers. We do not use analytics or advertising tools.

Sensitive data

The sessions you log are about your sexual life, which the law treats as a special category of personal data. We process them only because you choose to log them, to show your own stats and, unless you turn sharing off, to show them to your friends and in duels and crews. By creating an account and logging sessions you give explicit consent to this. You can withdraw consent at any time by erasing your data or deleting your account.

Who can see your data

  • You. Everything in your account.
  • Your friends see your sessions and stats, unless you turn sharing off in Settings. Sharing is on for new accounts. With sharing off, they see only your username and bio.
  • Your crew, if you join one, sees how many sessions each member logged toward the crew goal.
  • Duel opponents see your result for the stat the duel is about. Duels are only possible with sharing on.
  • Other users can find you by username, unless you turn off "Findable by username" in Settings.
  • Everyone, as totals only: the number of members, members active this week and sessions logged overall. These numbers never show who did what.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in and account emails such as confirmation and password reset.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when you load the site.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and the sign-in library to your browser, so they also see your IP address when the app loads.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. They act on our behalf and do not get your sessions for their own use.

Guest mode and your device

If you use Creamer as a guest, your sessions stay in your browser's storage on that device and are never sent to us. If you later create an account, they are moved into it. The app also keeps your login, settings and a running timer in browser storage. We do not use cookies for tracking.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. In Settings, under Data:

  • Copy my data gives you a copy of everything in your account.
  • Erase my data removes your sessions and progress but keeps the account.
  • Delete account removes your account and everything linked to it from the database straight away.

Backups held by our database provider expire on its own schedule.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself in Settings. For anything else, write to privacy@creamer.ink. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Adults only

Creamer is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Database rules let each account read only its own data and what friends have chosen to share. You can add a PIN lock to the app in Settings. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password and don't reuse it elsewhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how your sessions are used, we will tell you in the app first.
