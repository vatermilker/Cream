          Session

          Common questions

          What is Creamer?

          A private tracker. You log sessions with a rating, a from and to time, a yes or no and optional tags. The app turns them into stats, streaks, challenges and rewards. It stores numbers and times only.

          How do I add a session?

          Tap Log a session on the Home screen. Set the rating and times, pick tags, then save. Defaults come from Settings.

          How do I remove a session?

          On Home tap Show more, or open All sessions. Tap Remove on the session, then tap again to confirm.

          What is a crew?

          A crew is you and up to 7 friends sharing one tank. Every session anyone in the crew logs this week fills it. Fill it to the goal before Monday and everyone gets an award with the crew's name on it. Crew members see how many sessions each member logged this week, even if their stats are private. You can be in one crew at a time.

          What are surprise events?

          Events pop up at random times and last from one hour to a whole day. When one is live it shows on Home with its rule and a countdown. Log a session that fits the rule while it is live and you get the bonus. Everyone gets the same events at the same time.

          What are LoadDrops?

          LoadDrops (LDs for short) are the currency. You earn them from sessions, streaks, challenges and badges. Your balance is the white drop at the top. Spend LDs in the Shop: open Rewards, then Shop.

          What are XP and levels?

          XP counts every drop you have ever earned, spent or not. Levels use XP, so spending LDs never lowers your level.

          Who can see my stats?

          Only you, unless you pick "My friends" under Privacy. Then your accepted friends can see your sessions.

          How do friends and duels work?

          Open Friends and type a username. They have to accept. Duels and the weekly league need friends whose stats are set to "My friends".

          Can I change the word used in the app?

          Yes. Pick or type a word under Wording in Settings. It changes everywhere in the app on your screen.

          I forgot my password

          Log out, type your email on the login screen and tap Forgot password. A reset link arrives by email.

          How do I erase my data or delete my account?

          Both buttons are under Data in Settings. Each needs a second tap to confirm.

          Questions

          Good to know

          Is it free?

          Yes. There is nothing to pay.

          Do I need an account?

          No. You can try it as a guest. Guest sessions stay on your device, and they carry over if you create an account later.

          What do you store?

          Your email, username, an optional name and short bio, the awards you pin, and for each session the times, rating, whether it was released and any tags. No photos, contacts or location. Details are in the privacy policy.

          Who can see my sessions?

          Friends you accept can see your stats. Turn sharing off any time in Settings, and they only see your name, bio and pinned awards.

          What are awards?

          3D trophies you earn for streaks, milestones, duel wins and full crew tanks. Open one to spin it and see when and how you got it. Some are limited editions that can only be earned on certain dates.

          Do I need my real name?

          No. Your username is how friends add you. The name on your profile is optional and can be anything.

          Can I delete my account?

          Yes, from Settings. It removes your account and your sessions.

          Does it work on a computer?

          Yes. It works in any browser on a phone or a computer, and you can add it to your home screen.
