          Session

          Common questions

          What is Creamer?

          A private tracker. You log entries with a rating, a from and to time, a yes or no and optional tags. The app turns them into stats, streaks, challenges and rewards. It stores numbers and times only.

          How do I add an entry?

          Tap Log a load on the Home screen. Set the rating and times, pick tags, then save. Defaults come from Settings.

          How do I remove an entry?

          On Home tap Show more, or open All entries. Tap Remove on the entry, then tap again to confirm.

          What is a crew?

          A crew is you and up to 7 friends sharing one tank. Every load anyone in the crew logs this week fills it. Fill it to the goal before Monday and everyone gets 100 LDs. Crew members see how many loads each member logged this week, even if their stats are private. You can be in one crew at a time.

          What are surprise events?

          Events pop up at random times and last from one hour to a whole day. When one is live it shows on Home with its rule and a countdown. Log a load that fits the rule while it is live and you get the bonus. Everyone gets the same events at the same time.

          What are LoadDrops?

          LoadDrops (LDs for short) are the currency. You earn them from entries, streaks, challenges and badges. Your balance is the white drop at the top. Spend LDs in the Shop: open Rewards, then Shop.

          What are XP and levels?

          XP counts every drop you have ever earned, spent or not. Levels use XP, so spending LDs never lowers your level.

          Who can see my stats?

          Only you, unless you pick "My friends" under Privacy. Then your accepted friends can see your entries.

          How do friends and duels work?

          Open Friends and type a username. They have to accept. Duels and the weekly league need friends whose stats are set to "My friends".

          Can I change the word used in the app?

          Yes. Pick or type a word under Wording in Settings. It changes everywhere in the app on your screen.

          I forgot my password

          Log out, type your email on the login screen and tap Forgot password. A reset link arrives by email.

          How do I erase my data or delete my account?

          Both buttons are under Data in Settings. Each needs a second tap to confirm.
