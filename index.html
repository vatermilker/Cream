          Session

          Common questions

          What is Creamer?

          A private tracker. You log entries with a rating, a from and to time, a yes or no and optional tags. The app turns them into stats, streaks, challenges and rewards. It stores numbers and times only.

          How do I add an entry?

          Tap the splash button at the bottom. Set the rating and times, pick tags, then save. Defaults come from Settings.

          How do I remove an entry?

          On Home tap Show more, or open All entries. Tap Remove on the entry, then tap again to confirm.

          What are drops?

          Drops are the currency. You earn them from entries, streaks, challenges and badges. Your balance is the droplet at the top. Spend drops in the Shop: open Rewards, then Shop.

          What are XP and levels?

          XP counts every drop you have ever earned, spent or not. Levels use XP, so spending drops never lowers your level.

          Who can see my stats?

          Only you, unless you pick "My friends" under Privacy. Then your accepted friends can see your entries.

          How do friends and duels work?

          Open Friends and type a username. They have to accept. Duels and the weekly league need friends whose stats are set to "My friends".

          Can I change the word used in the app?

          Yes. Pick or type a word under Wording in Settings. It changes everywhere in the app on your screen.

          I forgot my password

          Log out, type your email on the login screen and tap Forgot password. A reset link arrives by email.

          How do I erase my data or delete my account?

          Both buttons are under Data in Settings. Each needs a second tap to confirm.
